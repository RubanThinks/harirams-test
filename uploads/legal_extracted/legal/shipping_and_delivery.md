# Shipping & Delivery Policy

Hariram's Pure Cow Ghee is committed to delivering your order safely, hygienically, and in a timely manner.

We work with trusted logistics partners to ensure that every order reaches you in good condition.

---

# 1. Delivery Coverage

Currently, Hariram's delivers products only within the state of **Tamil Nadu, India**.

Delivery availability depends on the serviceability of the destination PIN code.

We plan to expand our delivery network to additional states in the future.

---

# 2. Order Processing

Orders are processed only after successful payment confirmation.

Normally:

- Orders received before **12:00 PM** (business days) are processed on the same day.
- Orders received after **12:00 PM** are processed on the next business day.
- Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be processed on the next working day.

Order processing times may vary during festivals, promotional campaigns, or unforeseen circumstances.

---

# 3. Packaging

Every Hariram's product is carefully packed to preserve its quality during transit.

Packaging is designed to:

- Protect the bottle during transportation
- Maintain hygiene
- Minimise the risk of leakage or breakage

---

# 4. Estimated Delivery Timeline

Estimated delivery timelines are provided for guidance only.

Typical delivery timelines are:

| Destination | Estimated Delivery |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Chennai | 1–2 Business Days after order processing |
| Other locations within Tamil Nadu | 2–5 Business Days after order processing|

Actual delivery times may vary depending on courier operations and local conditions.

---

# 5. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated automatically during checkout based on the delivery PIN code.

Shipping rates are determined according to Hariram's Shipping Rules and may vary depending on:

- Delivery location
- Applicable shipping zone
- Promotional offers
- Free shipping campaigns (if applicable)

The applicable shipping charge will always be displayed before payment confirmation.

---

# 6. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, we will send shipment details through the communication channels available for your order.

Depending on the information provided during checkout, you may receive:

- Order Confirmation
- Shipping Confirmation
- Courier Partner Details
- Tracking Number
- Delivery Updates

Notifications may be sent via:

- WhatsApp
- SMS
- Email

---

# 7. Delivery Attempts

Our logistics partner may make more than one delivery attempt if the customer is unavailable.

If delivery cannot be completed because:

- nobody is available to receive the parcel,
- the delivery address is incorrect,
- the contact number is unreachable,

the shipment may be returned to Hariram's.

Additional shipping charges may apply for re-dispatch.

---

# 8. Delivery Address Changes

Customers may request a delivery address change only **before the order has been dispatched**.

Once the order has been handed over to the courier partner, address changes may not be possible.

---

# 9. Damaged Package at Delivery

Customers are encouraged to inspect the package upon delivery.

If the package appears to be:

- damaged,
- leaking,
- tampered with,

please:

- take clear photographs of the package,
- retain the packaging materials,
- contact Hariram's Customer Support within **48 hours** of delivery.

Any quality-related concerns will be handled according to our Returns & Refund Policy.

---

# 10. Delivery Delays

While we strive to deliver orders within the estimated timelines, delays may occasionally occur due to circumstances beyond our reasonable control, including:

- Heavy rainfall
- Floods
- Natural disasters
- Public holidays
- Festival rush
- Government restrictions
- Transportation disruptions
- Courier network delays

Hariram's will make reasonable efforts to keep customers informed of significant delays.

---

# 11. Delivery Failure

A delivery may fail due to:

- Incorrect delivery address
- Incorrect PIN code
- Customer unavailable
- Contact number unreachable
- Restricted delivery location

If the shipment is returned to Hariram's, we will contact the customer to discuss the available options.

Any additional shipping charges for re-dispatch shall be communicated before processing.

---

# 12. International Shipping

Currently, Hariram's ships only within Tamil Nadu, India.

International shipping is not available at this time.

# Payment Policy

Hariram's Pure Cow Ghee is committed to providing a safe, secure, and convenient payment experience for all customers.

All online payments are processed through authorised payment service providers using industry-standard security measures.

---

# 1. Accepted Payment Methods

Customers may make payments using one or more of the following methods, depending on availability at checkout:

- UPI
- Credit Cards
- Debit Cards
- Net Banking
- Wallets supported by the payment gateway
- Other payment methods offered by the payment gateway

The payment methods displayed during checkout are the only payment methods currently supported.

---

# 2. Payment Gateway

All payments are processed by trusted third-party payment gateway providers.

Hariram's does not directly process or store sensitive payment credentials.

Payment gateways use secure encryption and comply with applicable industry security standards.

---

# 3. Payment Confirmation

An order is considered confirmed only after:

- Successful payment authorisation, and
- Confirmation from the payment gateway.

Customers will receive an Order Confirmation after successful payment.

---

# 4. Failed Payments

A payment may fail for several reasons, including:

- Insufficient funds
- Bank authorisation failure
- UPI timeout
- Network interruption
- Technical issues with the payment provider

If payment fails, no order will be confirmed until a successful payment is received.

Customers may attempt the payment again.

---

# 5. Payment Security

For your protection:

- Hariram's never stores your credit or debit card details.
- Hariram's never stores your CVV.
- Hariram's never stores your UPI PIN.
- Sensitive payment information is handled only by certified payment service providers.

Customers should never share OTPs, passwords, or banking credentials with anyone.

---

# 6. Refunds

Where a refund is approved in accordance with our Returns & Refund Policy, the refund will normally be credited through the original payment method used for the purchase.

Depending on the payment provider or bank, refunds may take approximately **5–10 business days** to reflect in the customer's account.

---

# 7. Payment Disputes

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly or notice any discrepancy relating to your payment, please contact Hariram's Customer Support as soon as possible.

We will work with the payment gateway provider to investigate the matter and provide an appropriate resolution.

---

# 8. Pricing Currency

All prices displayed on this website are in **Indian Rupees (INR ₹)**.

Applicable taxes and shipping charges, where applicable, will be shown during checkout before payment is completed.

---

# 9. Changes to this Policy

Hariram's reserves the right to update this Payment Policy from time to time.

The latest version will always be available on this website.

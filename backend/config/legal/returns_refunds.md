# Returns & Refund Policy

At Hariram's Pure Cow Ghee, we are committed to delivering safe, high-quality food products to our customers.

Because our products are food items intended for human consumption, this Returns & Refund Policy has been designed in accordance with hygiene, food safety, and quality standards.

---

# 1. General Return Policy

Hariram's Pure Cow Ghee products are **not eligible for return** once delivered.

We do not accept returns because:

- Food products cannot be resold once delivered.
- Product safety and quality cannot be guaranteed after delivery.
- Returns may compromise hygiene and food safety.

Customers are therefore requested to purchase carefully before placing an order.

---

# 2. Damaged or Defective Products

Although every product undergoes quality checks before dispatch, if you believe that you have received a product that is defective or damaged, please contact us.

Examples include:

- Damaged bottle or container
- Broken safety seal
- Product leakage during transit
- Manufacturing defect
- Incorrect product delivered

---

# 3. Reporting a Quality Concern

Customers should notify Hariram's **within 48 hours of delivery**.

Please provide:

- Order Number
- Customer Name
- Contact Number
- Clear photographs & video of:
  - Product
  - Packaging
  - Batch Number
  - Manufacturing Date
  - Expiry Date
  - Shipping Box (if damaged)
- A brief description of the issue.

Providing complete information helps us investigate the matter quickly.

---

# 4. Investigation Process

Every reported complaint is individually reviewed.

Hariram's may conduct one or more of the following:

- Review customer photographs & videos
- Verify batch production records
- Inspect manufacturing quality records
- Consult our quality assurance team
- Coordinate with courier partners (if transit damage is suspected)
- Request additional information from the customer

Where necessary, Hariram's may arrange to collect the product for laboratory or internal quality examination.

---

# 5. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are **not automatic**.

A refund will be approved only if Hariram's concludes, after investigation, that:

- the product contains a verified manufacturing defect, or
- the product was damaged before delivery due to circumstances under Hariram's responsibility, or
- the incorrect product was supplied.

The decision of Hariram's, based on the investigation and available evidence, shall determine the outcome of the claim.

---

# 6. Situations Where Refunds Will Not Be Approved

Refund requests will generally not be approved for:

- Change of mind
- Taste preference
- Aroma preference
- Colour variations naturally occurring in ghee
- Texture variations that occur naturally
- Product ordered by mistake
- Incorrect shipping address provided by customer
- Delayed complaint beyond the reporting period
- Improper storage after delivery
- Damage caused after delivery

---

# 7. Refund Method

If a refund is approved, it will normally be processed through the original payment method used for the purchase.

Depending on the payment provider or bank, refunds may take **5–10 business days** to appear in the customer's account.

---

# 8. Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before dispatch.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

If Hariram's is unable to fulfil an order, a full refund will be issued.
